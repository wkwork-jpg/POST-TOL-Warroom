PostTOL-Download

PostTOL-Download is a personal internal tool that downloads two Google Sheets (Postpaid 101 and Register True Online form responses) as Excel files once a day, so a local dashboard on the owner's own computer can be refreshed automatically.

It is used only by its owner. It requests read-only access to Google Drive, does not modify or share any file, and does not send data to any third party.
