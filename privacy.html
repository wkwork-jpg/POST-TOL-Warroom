Privacy Policy — PostTOL-Download

Last updated: 10 November 2026

What the app accesses

The app uses the Google Drive read-only scope to find and export two specific Google Sheets that the signed-in user can already view.

How data is used

Exported files are saved only on the owner's own computer and used to build a local dashboard. The app does not modify, delete or share any Google Drive file.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, transferred or disclosed to any third party.

Storage and removal

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
